# BED-038 x03 RC-02: static adversarial review matrix

This is a source-level expectation matrix, not an executed test or runner observation. It documents the proposed fixed intervention plan and the parent and source-independent teardown predicates. Independent review and later separately authorized runner evidence remain required.

For every row, assume all other conditions are valid unless the row says otherwise. The sole intended exception is the x03 preflight `child-timeout` control: role `control`, ordinal 10, unit `b38-x03-control-10.service`, three-second runtime, no injected mount, and the precommitted `systemd_runtime_timeout` plan with `kill_signal=9`, `expected_code=2`, `expected_status=9`, and `readiness_absent=true`. Effective policy must show `KillSignal=SIGKILL`, `KillMode=control-group`, `SendSIGKILL=yes`, `FinalKillSignal=SIGKILL`, the reviewed deadlines, and matching retained unit and invocation identities. Readiness files must be absent before creation and subsequently pass the bounded ownership, format and consistency checks. The unit must have a terminal state, no parent stop attempt or pre-stop terminal record, and a captured empty dedicated-slice witness.

| --- | --- | --- |

The fixed plan and recorded main-process tuple describe the expected intervention and observed manager result. They do **not** prove that the timeout caused the particular signal or establish the signal received by each descendant. An empty slice is a cessation witness, not a per-process signal record. Actual systemd behavior and the resulting evidence remain unobserved here.
