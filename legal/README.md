# Bowfin Contributor Agreements

The Bowfin Project requires a signed contributor assignment agreement before it
merges externally authored copyrightable material.

## Current rights-holder

The current receiving party is:

> **Gregory M. Krsak, an individual**

The agreements permit transfer to a future Bowfin entity, subject to their
assignment terms. Establishing that entity will require updated forms for new
contributors and a documented transfer of the existing project rights and
agreements.

## Available forms

- [Individual Contributor Assignment Agreement](INDIVIDUAL_CONTRIBUTOR_ASSIGNMENT_AGREEMENT.md)
- [Entity Contributor Assignment Agreement](ENTITY_CONTRIBUTOR_ASSIGNMENT_AGREEMENT.md)

Use the Individual agreement only when the individual owns the contribution and
has authority to assign it. Use the Entity agreement when an employer or other
legal entity owns or controls the contribution. An authorized representative must
sign for an entity.

## Private execution process

Open a repository issue requesting private agreement-submission instructions.
Do not place signed agreements, street addresses, signatures, or other personal
information in a public issue, pull request, or repository commit.

An agreement is considered on file only after the contributor and Gregory M.
Krsak have executed it and the project owner has confirmed its recorded scope in
writing. Pull-request templates, commit sign-offs, and issue comments are not
substitutes for the agreement.

Executed agreements and contributor identity records must be retained privately
with an auditable association to the contributor's GitHub identity and, for an
entity, the contributing accounts it authorizes.

## Non-owner and third-party material

Do not submit material owned by another person or entity unless the rights-holder
has executed the appropriate agreement or the project owner has approved a
compatible upstream license and the exact provenance in writing. Identify such
material before submitting any patch.

Material created with generative-AI or code-generation tools must be disclosed as
required by `CONTRIBUTING.md`. Do not submit it when you cannot grant the rights or
make the representations required by the applicable agreement.
