# Bowfin Trademark Policy

## Purpose

The Bowfin source code is open source. The **Bowfin** name and the canonical
fish-and-`BOWFIN` logo identify the official language, compiler, toolchain,
documentation, and releases stewarded by the Bowfin Project. Open-source
permission to use the software does not grant permission to present a modified
or independent product as an official Bowfin product.

The Bowfin name and canonical logo are trademarks of **Gregory M. Krsak**, the
current Bowfin rights-holder. They are expected to transfer to a future Bowfin
entity when that entity is established. The registered-trademark symbol must not
be used unless and until the applicable mark is registered.

## Uses that do not require separate permission

Subject to applicable law, you may:

- make truthful, non-misleading statements that software is written in, built
  with, derived from, or compatible with Bowfin;
- refer to the official Bowfin language, compiler, documentation, repository,
  or releases;
- reproduce the Bowfin name as required by the license, attribution, or NOTICE;
- link to the official Bowfin repository or website; and
- use the unmodified name or logo in news, commentary, teaching, criticism, or
  community discussion without implying sponsorship or endorsement.

These uses must not suggest that Gregory M. Krsak or the Bowfin Project sponsors,
certifies, endorses, or maintains a product when that is not true.

## Modified distributions and independent implementations

You may modify, fork, and redistribute the software under the repository's
license. Unless separately authorized in writing, however, a modified
distribution or independent implementation must:

- use a distinct product and executable identity;
- avoid using **Bowfin** or the Bowfin logo as its primary product name, logo,
  package identity, domain identity, or release identity;
- state clearly that it is unofficial and independently maintained whenever it
  describes itself as Bowfin-derived or Bowfin-compatible; and
- avoid any presentation likely to imply official status, compatibility
  certification, or project endorsement.

The factual phrase **"compatible with Bowfin"** is acceptable when accurate and
not presented as an official certification.

## Official status

Only releases, specifications, compatibility designations, and other materials
expressly published or authorized through the Bowfin Project's canonical
governance may claim official Bowfin status. A fork, standards effort, package,
or implementation does not become official merely because it is derived from,
compatible with, or discussed in connection with Bowfin.

## Permission requests

Open a repository issue requesting private trademark-contact instructions. Do
not post personal information or confidential materials in a public issue.

This policy describes the project's trademark-use position. It does not limit
rights that applicable law permits without authorization.
